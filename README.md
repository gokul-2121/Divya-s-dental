# Dr. Divya's Multispeciality Dental Clinic - Official Website

Modern, responsive website built for **Dr. Divya's Multispeciality Dental Clinic** located in Kazhakkoottam, Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala.

## 🌟 Key Features
- **Pristine Clinical Theme**: Crisp white aesthetic (`#FFFFFF`) with fresh medical ocean & sky blue accents (`#0284C7`, `#0369A1`, `#F0F7FF`).
- **Real Google Business Photos**: Authentic photos of the clinic building & signboard above Shop & Save, reception lounge & official specialists board, modern operatory dental chair, and active doctor treatment.
- **Picture Representation of Services**: Each dental treatment (Microscopic RCT, Implants, Braces & Aligners, Laser Whitening, Pediatric Care, 3D Scans) features procedure action photography and clinical details.
- **Doctor in Action Showcase**: Visuals of specialists actively performing procedures (operating microscopes, surgical loupes, sterile autoclave instruments).
- **Client Photo Customizer**: Built-in self-service photo manager allowing the doctor to replace photos anytime via local upload, image URL, or curated presets.
- **Customer Reviews**: Google 4.9 ★ rating badge (274+ reviews), authentic patient testimonials, category filtering, and interactive review submission.
- **Kazhakkoottam Location & Navigation**: Address details, interactive Google Map, working hours table, and one-click Google Maps driving directions.
- **WhatsApp Direct Connect**: Instant appointment scheduling linked to `+91 85938 24501`.

## 🚀 Running Locally
Open terminal in the project directory:
```bash
node server.js
```
Visit `http://localhost:3000/` in your browser.
